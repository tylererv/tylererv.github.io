Edge-Generated Pothole Detection

A real-time road maintenance research workflow that turns dashcam footage into localized pothole events, review clips, and AI-assisted severity assessments.

Abstract PDF Poster
01

Research Focus

This research explores how edge-friendly computer vision can identify potholes from vehicle dashcam footage and convert raw frame detections into practical maintenance reports. The workflow uses YOLOv8 for pothole detection, OpenCV for preprocessing and clip generation, Gemini Flash for severity analysis, and Supabase for structured event storage.

The goal is to make pothole detection more useful to transportation teams by producing organized, location-aware outputs instead of large volumes of raw frame-by-frame predictions.

Poster: edge-generated localization and identification of road potholes via real-time vehicle dashcam footage.

02

Pipeline

  • Collect and preprocess dashcam video with OpenCV.
  • Run YOLOv8 object detection to identify potholes in roadway footage.
  • Post-process detections into event-level pothole reports and short evidence clips.
  • Use Gemini Flash to analyze pothole severity from generated clips.
  • Store structured results in Supabase for dashboard review and future GIS integration.
03

Research Value

The workflow shifts pothole detection from isolated model outputs toward actionable maintenance evidence. By combining preprocessing, detection, event creation, and severity review, the system can help reduce noisy repeated outputs and give reviewers cleaner material for prioritizing road repairs.
